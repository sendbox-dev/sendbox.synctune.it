SendBox Fleet

Shared governance for SendBox teams.

SendBox Fleet is the shared governance service for SendBox governed sessions: operator-signed policy profiles shared across users and hosts, project roles, session admission, credential grants and a hash-chained central audit log. Provider credentials never reach it; they stay in the SendBox host broker.
